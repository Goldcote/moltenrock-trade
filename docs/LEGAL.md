# Legal pages in a MoltenRock Trade portal

Every portal has three public pages, linked in the footer of every storefront page:

| Page | Address | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| General terms for trade customers | `/legal/terms` | B2B terms (scope, account, prices, contract, delivery, QR-bill payment, inspection/defects, liability, law and jurisdiction) |
| Privacy notice | `/legal/privacy` | Swiss FADP / EU GDPR notice (controller, data, purposes, processors, transfers, retention, cookies, rights) |
| Legal notice (imprint) | `/legal/imprint` | Company, address, contact, UID / VAT number |

## Built-in templates or your own pages

- **Built-in (default):** written in German, French, Italian and English and filled from the shop's own details and settings. Payment terms, minimum order, approval amount, cancel window, VAT registration and the place of jurisdiction (the shop's town) always match how the portal actually works. When the email provider is set up, the privacy notice names it.
- **Your own:** the owner can link the pages the shop already has (Setup → *Before your first trade customer* → *Terms and privacy*). Links then point there directly.
- Only a signed-in **owner** can change these links or confirm the pages. Agents can read them, never change them.

**The templates are a starting point, not legal advice.** The owner sees that note on the pages, and confirming them in Setup says so too. Have them reviewed, for example by a Swiss lawyer or your trade association, before real trade customers use the portal.

## Acceptance is recorded

- Customers who **apply** tick "I accept the terms and the privacy notice" (both linked). The time and *which* terms (your page's address, or the template version such as `template-2026-09`) are stored with the customer.
- Customers **invited** by the agent never saw that form, so they accept once on their first sign-in, before they can see prices or order.
- The customers export (Exports → *Trade customers*) lists when each customer accepted.

## Not covered

Consumer (B2C) sales, distance-selling rules and export outside Switzerland: the portal is B2B and ships within Switzerland.
