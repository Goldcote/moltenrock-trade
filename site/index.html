FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Do I need a payment provider?

No. Trade customers pay by Swiss QR-bill, Net 14 by default. No card fees, no payment integration.

Can the agent break my shop?

No. It only ever reads your shop: with a read-only key on WooCommerce, or your public product pages on any other shop. It can’t touch your bank details, can’t make a price live, and can’t approve customers or large orders – those wait for you.

Where is my data?

In your own Cloudflare account, in Cloudflare’s EU jurisdiction. We never host it and never see it.

Do I need to code?

No. Click “Deploy to Cloudflare”, open your new address, sign up. The portal creates its own database and keys.

Which agents work?

Any agent that speaks MCP — for example Claude. Others can use the REST API; everything is described in llms.txt.

Which shop systems?

Any. WooCommerce connects directly with a read-only key, including Polylang and WPML translations. Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, your own website or a spreadsheet: your agent reads your products and their prices, and you confirm every price before anyone can order.
